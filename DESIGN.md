# Smart Clinic interface

Approved direction: light clinical workspace; blue/teal accents; three role portals.
Operate mode: login first, then the role's primary task; no marketing hero.
Palette: background #f2f6f8, surface #fff, text #183340, muted #526c78, primary #086b78, border #cfdee4.
System Segoe UI at 16px; headings 30px/22px. Single column login; broad workspace with aligned fields, clear labels and data tables. 24px section spacing and 12px field spacing. Primary actions filled teal, secondary actions outlined, errors dark red. Keyboard focus outlined, loading button disabled, empty results explained. Synthetic-data notice visible.
First viewport: clinic wordmark, role tabs, portal title and login form with one sign-in action. Signature interaction: role tabs change the portal and scope of the authenticated workspace; patient search shows a doctor with real availability and booking action.
